# Distribution — start before the app is approved

## The rule

Don't invent formats. The screen-time category has years of proven short-form content. Copy the
*format*, change the story, post at volume, and judge by the tier ladder, not by views.

## Tier ladder (how we judge every video)

| Tier | Signal | What to fix if stuck here |
|---|---|---|
| 0 | Low views | Hook / first frame. |
| 1 | Views | Product appears too late or is unclear. Show the fold + shield in the first 2 seconds. |
| 2 | Views + engagement | Content is entertaining but not about the app. Make the demo the story. |
| 3 | + Downloads | Onboarding / paywall. Marketing did its job. |
| 4 | + Conversions | Scale this concept. |
| 5 | + Low churn | The business works. Do more of exactly this. |

## Formats that already work in this category (make our versions)

1. **Screen-recording demo** — phone locks, tries to open TikTok, hits the block screen. 5–8s.
2. **"My screen time was X, now it's Y"** — before/after iOS Screen Time screenshots.
3. **Slideshow / text-on-image** — 5 slides: "Signs your phone is running your life" → slide 5 is the app.
4. **POV / skit** — "POV: you try to doomscroll at 1am but your phone is clammed up."
5. **Founder build-in-public** — "I built an app that turns the iPhone Duo into a brick. Day 3."
6. **Reaction to stat** — "17 years of your life" stat with the app as the answer.

## 20 hooks to test (first frame text)

1. The iPhone Duo made my screen time worse. So I built this.
2. Fold it. It's locked. That's the app.
3. Your phone is a slot machine. This is the off switch.
4. I get 27 days a year back with one button.
5. POV: 1am, you unfold your phone, and Instagram is clammed up.
6. Stop deleting Instagram every Sunday.
7. 96 times. That's how often you unlocked yesterday.
8. This is the block screen that actually stopped me.
9. The $2,000 iPhone Duo vs. a $40 app that makes you close it.
10. I don't have willpower. I clam up.
11. What happens when you try to open TikTok during a Clam session.
12. Brick costs $59 and you'll lose it. Your phone is the brick.
13. Day 1 vs Day 30 of clamming up my phone.
14. The one iPhone setting nobody uses, done right.
15. Screen time apps are bloated. This one has one button.
16. Fold. Focus. Unfold. Done.
17. My streak is 41 days. Here's the app.
18. Opal wants $100/year. Here's the $40 version.
19. This is the only app I opened today (that's the point).
20. Watch what my phone shows when it's closed.

## Creator brief (paste to small creators)

- Who: 5k–50k followers, at least two videos over 500k views, native short-form talkers.
- Ask: 3 videos over 2 weeks, formats 1, 2 or 4 above. We provide: promo code, hooks list,
  3 reference videos, screen recordings, the share card.
- Pay: flat $150–300 per video plus a $2 bounty per trial start via their code (tracked with
  App Store offer codes / promo links).
- Don't ask them to be original. Ask them to make *their* version of what already works.

## Duo launch week (Oct 16 preorders, Oct 23 delivery)

- Every tech creator is unboxing. Pitch 20 of them: "the app that makes the fold the feature".
- Post daily: "first 5 apps for the iPhone Duo", "iPhone Duo focus mode nobody's talking about".
- Reddit: r/iphone, r/digitalminimalism, r/nosurf, r/productivity. Value-first posts, not links.

## Paid (only after organic reads)

- Take the 3–5 concepts that produced *trials*, not views.
- $20/day per concept on TikTok Spark Ads first (boosting the organic post keeps social proof).
- Kill anything above $3 CPI after $100 spend. Scale anything under $2.

# The first 10 launch videos: shot lists

Every one of these is a format that already works in the screen-time category. The job is to
record them, not to improve them. Record all ten in one afternoon with a second phone (or a Mac
screen recording of the simulator for the app parts), then post 1–2 a day across two accounts.

Rules for all ten:
- Product visible within 2 seconds. No intro, no logo.
- Text hook on the first frame, 6 words or fewer.
- 7–15 seconds. Longer only if the format needs it.
- CTA is the app name on screen at the end, nothing else. Don't say "link in bio".
- Captions on. Most people watch on mute.

---

### 1. The demo (the one everything else points back to)
**Hook text:** "Fold it. It's locked."
**Shots:**
1. (0–1s) Hand taps the yellow "Clam up" button. Hard cut.
2. (1–3s) Phone folds shut (or locks). Outer screen / lock screen shows the countdown.
3. (3–5s) Phone opens. Thumb taps Instagram.
4. (5–7s) Block screen: "Instagram is clammed up. 24 min left."
5. (7–8s) Phone folds shut again. End card: app name.
**Why it works:** Nothing to explain. This is the App Store preview too.

### 2. Screen time before/after
**Hook text:** "My screen time. 30 days apart."
**Shots:**
1. iOS Screen Time screenshot: 6h 12m daily average. Hold 2s.
2. Swipe transition. Screenshot: 2h 40m.
3. Cut to the app's home screen with a 30-day streak.
4. End card.
**Note:** Needs a real 30 days. Start recording your own screen time today.

### 3. POV: 1am
**Hook text:** "POV: it's 1am and you unfold your phone"
**Shots:**
1. Dark room, phone lights up your face. Unfold.
2. Tap TikTok. Block screen appears.
3. Look at camera, defeated. Fold it shut.
4. End card: "Clam. Go to sleep."

### 4. Slideshow: 5 signs
**Hook slide:** "5 signs your phone owns you"
**Slides:**
1. You unlock it to check the time, then 20 minutes disappear.
2. You reach for it during every pause: line, elevator, red light.
3. You've deleted Instagram "for good" more than once.
4. Your screen time report makes you feel sick every Sunday.
5. Slide 5: the app's block screen. "This is the fix. Fold it."
**Music:** whatever's trending that week. Slideshows live on the sound.

### 5. iPhone Duo made it worse
**Hook text:** "The iPhone Duo made my screen time worse. So I built this."
**Shots:**
1. Unboxed Duo, open, big screen scrolling. 2s.
2. Talking head, one sentence: "Bigger screen, more doomscrolling. So I made closing it the point."
3. Demo shots 1–4 from video #1.
4. End card.
**Timing:** Post the week of Oct 23, when the Duo lands.

### 6. Brick vs. Clam
**Hook text:** "Brick is $59 and you'll lose it."
**Shots:**
1. A Brick (or any NFC tag) on a table. Hand picks it up, looks around, "where is it".
2. Cut: the app. "Your phone is the brick." Fold it.
3. Block screen.
4. End card: "$40/year. Nothing to lose."
**Note:** Keep it playful. Brick is a good product; we're not trashing it.

### 7. The hold-to-quit
**Hook text:** "What happens when you try to quit early"
**Shots:**
1. Active session ring. Thumb presses "Hold 10s to give up".
2. Red bar fills slowly. Caption counts: 9… 8… 7…
3. Thumb lets go at 4. Bar resets. "Giving up resets your 12-day streak."
4. Fold the phone instead.
**Why:** The friction is the feature, and people love watching someone almost fail.

### 8. Action Button
**Hook text:** "I mapped my Action Button to brick my phone"
**Shots:**
1. Settings → Action Button → Shortcut → "Clam up". 2s.
2. Press the Action Button. Phone locks, Live Activity appears.
3. Try to open an app. Block screen.
4. End card.

### 9. Days back (the reveal)
**Hook text:** "You spend 68 days a year on your phone"
**Shots:**
1. Screen recording of the onboarding reveal: number counts up to 68.
2. "People who lock their apps cut that by 40%." → 27 days back.
3. Cut to demo shot 2 (fold, countdown).
4. End card.

### 10. Founder, day 1
**Hook text:** "Day 1 of trying to get 1,000 people to close their phones"
**Shots:**
1. Talking head, phone in hand. "I built an app that locks your apps when you fold your phone. Here's the whole thing." (demo plays picture-in-picture)
2. "I'll post every day until 1,000 people are using it. Day 1: 0."
3. End card.
**Why:** Build-in-public series give you a reason to post daily and a story people follow.

---

## What to measure after the first 20 posts

Per video: views, profile taps, App Store page views (App Store Connect → Analytics → Sources),
installs, trial starts. Put them in one spreadsheet. The video with the best **installs per 1,000
views** wins, regardless of total views. Make ten more of that one.

What Clam does

Clam uses Apple's Screen Time framework to restrict access to apps you select, for a period you start. It is a tool to support your own choices. It is not a security product and does not guarantee that restrictions cannot be bypassed, for example by deleting the app or changing Screen Time settings.

Subscriptions and purchases

  • Clam offers auto-renewing subscriptions (yearly and monthly) and a one-time lifetime purchase, sold through Apple's App Store.
  • A free trial, where offered, converts to a paid subscription at the end of the trial unless cancelled at least 24 hours before it ends.
  • Subscriptions renew automatically at the price shown at purchase unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the end of the current period.
  • You can manage or cancel in Settings → Apple ID → Subscriptions on your device. Refunds are handled by Apple under its policies.

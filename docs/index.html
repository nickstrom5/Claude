Questions

Straight answers, including the unflattering ones.

How do I block apps on my iPhone with Clam?

Pick the apps once. Then tap Clam up, choose a length from 5 minutes to 4 hours, and lock your phone. Until the timer ends, the apps you picked open to a block screen instead of a feed.

Can Clam block TikTok, Instagram and YouTube?

Yes. You choose from Apple's own app picker, so any app on your iPhone can go on the list: TikTok, Instagram, YouTube, X, games, whatever it is for you. You can change the list any time.

Is it real blocking, or a reminder I can tap past?

Real blocking. Clam uses Apple's Screen Time API, the same system behind the built-in app limits. The block screen has one button, and it says OK, I'll wait. There is no ignore-limit button.

Can I just delete the app to get around it?

Yes. Every blocker on iOS has that limit, and any that claims otherwise is lying. Clam adds friction, not walls: a 10-second hold to quit and a streak you'll lose.

What if I need a locked app during a session?

Hold the give up button for 10 seconds. The session ends, your apps unlock, and your streak resets. That's the deal.

Does Clam see what I do in my apps?

No. Apple's Screen Time framework gives Clam opaque tokens for the apps you pick. Clam can't see which apps they are, can't read anything inside them, and never sends anything off your phone. There's no account and no server.

Why does it need Screen Time permission?

That's the only way to actually lock apps on iOS. Clam asks for it once, for you alone (not a family or child setting), and uses it only during a session you started.

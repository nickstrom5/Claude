Questions

Can I just delete the app to get around it?

Yes. Every blocker on iOS has that limit, and any that claims otherwise is lying. Clam adds friction, not walls: a 10-second hold to quit and a streak you'll lose. That's what changes behaviour.

Does Clam see what I do in my apps?

No. Apple's Screen Time framework gives Clam opaque tokens for the apps you pick. Clam can't see which apps they are, can't read anything inside them, and never sends anything off your phone. There's no account and no server. Privacy policy.

Why does it need Screen Time permission?

That's the only way to actually lock apps on iOS. Clam asks for it once, for you alone (not a family or child setting), and uses it only during a session you started.

What if I need a locked app during a session?

Hold the "give up" button for 10 seconds. The session ends, your apps unlock, and your streak resets. That's the deal.

Does it work without an iPhone Duo?

Yes. On every iPhone running iOS 17 or later the countdown is on the lock screen and in the Dynamic Island. The Duo just makes the ritual physical.
